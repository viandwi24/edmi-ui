# Code of Conduct

Edmi UI adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
by reference. The full text at that link applies to every space of this project: issues, pull requests, discussions
and any channel where you represent it.

## Summary

- Be welcoming, respectful and constructive. Assume good intent and give specific, kind feedback.
- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualised language, doxxing and trolling are not tolerated.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus on what is best for the community and the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at **viandwi24@pm.me**. Reports are handled privately and in good
faith. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or
permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge inappropriate, following the enforcement guidelines in the
Contributor Covenant.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
