## Elevation ✦

See the [Elevation guide](/edmi-ui/getting-started/elevation/) for the levels, roles and layered mode.

Suggestions are Buttons, so they follow the Button levels: flat by default, `sunken` (-1), `raised` (+1) and `floating` (+2).

## ✦ Edmi additions
- `variant="card"`: a larger tile with a lead-in line; `children` is the muted description.
