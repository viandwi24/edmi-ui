## ✦ Edmi additions
- Split Download button; the chevron opens copy link, open and similar actions.
- `state="generating"`: the title shimmers and the actions are hidden.
- `ArtifactCardThumbnail`: the top of page 1 as paper (always light, also in dark mode); `variant="slide"` is dark. It sits flush with the card edges.
