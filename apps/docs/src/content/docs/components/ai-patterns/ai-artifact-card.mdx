## Elevation ✦

The card are flat by default and take the surface level in layered mode (raised). `sunken` (-1) is a soft inset, `raised` (+1) a bevel and `floating` (+2) a bevel with a soft drop.

## ✦ Edmi additions
- Split Download button; the chevron opens copy link, open and similar actions.
- `state="generating"`: the title shimmers and the actions are hidden.
- `ArtifactCardThumbnail`: the top of page 1 as paper (always light, also in dark mode); `variant="slide"` is dark. It is an inset tile the size of the icon tile, clipped to its radius.
