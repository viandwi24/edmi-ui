Elevation system v4 (foundation). New tokens (`--bv-*` bevel, `--sk-*` sunken, `--r1-*` / `--fl-*` button faces), dark ladder B for the stone base, and the new Tailwind shadow utilities (`shadow-raised`, `shadow-floating`, `shadow-sunken`, `shadow-btn-raised-*`, `shadow-btn-float-*`, `shadow-pressed`, ...). New item `@edmi-ui/elevation` (`ElevationProvider`, `useElevation`, `resolveElevation`, `ROLE_LEVEL`) that components use to resolve their `elevation` prop; wrap an app or subtree in `<ElevationProvider mode="layered">` to give every role its default depth.

Migration (breaking, 0.x): the boolean `raised` prop is replaced by `elevation="auto" | "sunken" | "flat" | "raised" | "floating"` as components are updated (`raised` becomes `elevation="raised"`); hard lips are replaced by a bevel, and the legacy lip tokens stay only for compatibility. The `theme` item's base rule now targets `[data-elevation=raised], [data-elevation=floating]` instead of `[data-raised]`. Re-run `add @edmi-ui/theme --overwrite` and your component installs.
