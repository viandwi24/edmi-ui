Add the Edmi AI pack: items named `ai-<name>` (chat, agent, code, runtime, voice and workflow components plus Edmi additions) and the `ai-all` aggregate, installed into `components/ai/`. React ships AI · Chat (`ai-conversation`, `ai-message`, `ai-prompt-input`, `ai-suggestion`, `ai-attachments`, `ai-model-selector`, `ai-context`, `ai-shimmer`) and the `ai-use-controllable-state` hook; Vue and Svelte ports follow. `all` and `edmi` never include AI items.
